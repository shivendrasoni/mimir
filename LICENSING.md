## Earlier MIT releases

The following `mimir-ai` versions were published under the MIT License and retain those terms:

- 0.5.0
- 0.11.0
- 0.13.0
- 0.19.0

Those versions have been yanked from crates.io. Yanking prevents Cargo from selecting them for new dependency resolutions by default, but it does not delete their archives or revoke the MIT rights already granted with them. Existing locked dependencies can continue to resolve them.

Source revisions and release artifacts that included the MIT License remain available under the MIT terms included with those copies. Source snapshots and release artifacts that include the PolyForm Noncommercial License are offered under the PolyForm terms.
